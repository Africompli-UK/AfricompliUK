# AfriCompli UK — Roadmap

This document outlines AfriCompli UK's product development roadmap, business milestones, and international scaling strategy.

---

## Development Phases

### Phase 1 — Discovery and Product Definition *(Months 1–2)*

**Objective:** Finalise functional scope, compliance logic, and user journey.

**Deliverables:**
- Product Requirements Document (PRD)
- MVP wireframes and Figma prototype
- Compliance rules matrix (SRS 1 & 2, FCA, Companies Act, IFRS SDS)
- User journey and architecture diagrams
- Pilot client shortlist and outreach

---

### Phase 2 — MVP Build *(Months 3–5)*

**Objective:** Build a functional SaaS platform and AfriLaw™ beta.

**Deliverables:**
- Authentication and user account management
- Business profile questionnaire
- SRS/ESG applicability assessment engine
- Compliance checklist generator
- Evidence upload and repository
- Compliance heat map and readiness score
- Draft ESG readiness report builder
- Admin portal for internal review

---

### Phase 3 — Pilot Testing *(Months 6–7)*

**Objective:** Test usability, compliance relevance, and willingness to pay with 3–5 real businesses.

**Deliverables:**
- Pilot feedback report
- Improved MVP based on user findings
- Pricing validation evidence
- At least one testimonial or anonymised case study

**Pilot targets:**
- 3–5 African-founded or overseas SMEs across London, Manchester, Birmingham, Lincoln
- Sectors: fintech, professional services, logistics, import/export

---

### Phase 4 — Public Launch *(Months 8–12)*

**Objective:** Launch paid SaaS subscriptions and consulting services.

**Deliverables:**
- Live Starter, Growth, and Scale subscription tiers
- Customer onboarding materials
- Partner referral programme activated
- Consultancy terms and billing live
- Privacy notice and DPA published
- AfriLaw™ beta live on mobile (iOS/Android)

---

### Phase 5 — Scale and Integration *(Year 2)*

**Objective:** Expand features, partners, and API integrations.

**Deliverables:**
- API Data Hub live (Xero, QuickBooks, carbon-tracking, HR system connectors)
- Advanced reporting and audit simulator
- Partner portal for law firms and accelerators
- Multilingual AfriLaw™ beta (Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, French)
- Funding tracker and investor ESG pack builder

**Business targets:**
- 100 new clients
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR): £300k–£600k
- Partnerships live with TechNation, Foundervine, and 1+ law firm

---

### Phase 6 — International Expansion *(Year 3)*

**Objective:** Replicate the UK compliance model for selected international markets.

**Deliverables:**
- Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya localisation (SaaS modules + regional law firm partnerships)
- EU CSRD-aligned compliance module
- UAE and Singapore partnership agreements with ESG consultancies
- White-label partner package for multinational clients
- International legal rules matrix (cross-border disclosure obligations)

**Business targets:**
- 20 UK FTEs employed
- Profit projection: £600k–£2.2M (EBITDA 40%)
- 30+ UK jobs created by end of Year 3

---

## Three-Year Business Roadmap

### Year 1 — Foundation ("Build and Pilot")

- Deploy £40,000 seed capital for MVP and AfriLaw™ beta
- Recruit core team: 2 software engineers + 1 regulatory analyst
- Launch pilot with 3–5 African-founded fintechs
- Target: **30 paying clients** — projected revenue £100k–£352k

### Year 2 — Expansion ("Adopt and Educate")

- Full SaaS rollout with all modules
- Hire customer success, marketing, and sales team
- Partner with TechNation and Foundervine accelerators
- Expand from African-owned to all overseas companies
- Target: **100+ new clients** — ARR £300k–£975k

### Year 3 — Scale ("Automate and Export")

- Advanced AI models and full audit automation
- International expansion: Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya as first markets
- Secondary expansion: EU (CSRD), UAE, Singapore
- Target: **300 clients** — revenue £1M–£2.2M

## International Scaling Strategy

| Market | Entry Strategy | Synergy with UK HQ |
|---|---|---|
| Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya | Export SaaS modules, localise with regional law firms | Generates reciprocal data flows for UK clients with African operations |
| EU | CSRD-aligned compliance module | Demonstrates cross-continent scalability to EU markets |
| UAE and Singapore | Partnership with multinational ESG consultancies | Positions AfriCompli UK as a cross-continent ESG bridge |
