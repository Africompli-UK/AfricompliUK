# AfriCompli UK — Product Overview

## What We Are

AfriCompli UK is a **RegTech startup** building an AI-powered SaaS compliance platform and mobile application for African-founded and overseas businesses operating in the United Kingdom.

We are the first product to combine:

- AI-assisted UK sustainability reporting (SRS 1 & 2) applicability assessment
- ESG readiness scoring, evidence workflows, and report generation
- A mobile law companion (AfriLaw™) for diaspora founders
- Human consultant review for complex and high-stakes filings
- An education and partnership hub built around African and overseas business communities

---

## The Problem We Solve

### The Regulatory Backdrop

As of **1 January 2027**, the UK's Sustainability Reporting Standards (SRS 1 & 2) become mandatory. SRS 1 requires entities to disclose information on sustainability-related risks and opportunities. SRS 2 requires disclosure of climate-related risks and opportunities. Every business linked to a UK-listed entity — directly or through its supply chain — must make formal sustainability disclosures or face penalties and exclusion from funding.

### Why Overseas Businesses Struggle

| Challenge | Detail |
|---|---|
| Complex regulatory language | SRS and ESG rules are written in technical legalese inaccessible to non-lawyers |
| No specialist support | No affordable advisor focused on overseas founders' cross-border compliance context |
| Funding exclusion | ESG non-compliance blocks eligibility for green loans, grants, and investor access |
| Disconnected tools | Founders rely on scattered guides and expensive law firm advice |
| Dual adaptation | Overseas businesses must simultaneously internalise UK rules and reconcile them with home-country reporting requirements |

### Who Is Affected

- **Direct exposure:** Large overseas companies with 250+ employees or significant UK turnover face mandatory SRS filings
- **Indirect exposure:** SMEs supplying UK-listed or regulated companies face ESG data requests as part of value-chain reporting — even if not directly mandated
- **Scale:** Up to 2,186 of the 14,574 overseas companies registered in the UK (Companies House, FY 2025) are materially affected

## Our Solution — AfriCompli Intelligent Compliance Framework (AICF)

AfriCompli's platform is built around six integrated modules:

### 1. E-Compliance Cloud (SaaS Platform)

An AI NLP engine that scans UK regulations, extracts obligations, and auto-maps them to each user's business profile.

**Core functions:**
- Real-time compliance checklist generator
- Rule-update notifications (FCA, BEIS, Companies House feeds)
- Automated data input from CRM and accounting systems (Xero, QuickBooks)
- Automated Report Builder — PDF, Excel, and XML outputs
- ESG R&D Tracker — recommends environment-impact projects for double-materiality criteria and funding eligibility
- Compliance heat map across 8 dimensions (Governance, Climate Risk, Energy Data, Social Impact, Supplier ESG, Data Protection, Sustainability Strategy, Funding Readiness)

### 2. AfriLaw™ Mobile Application

A consumer-facing law application providing on-demand legal intelligence to diaspora founders.

**Features:**
- Smart Chat AI trained on SRS FAQs and model contracts
- Speech-to-text compliance queries
- Push-alert system for filing deadlines and risk thresholds
- Contract templates, disclosure clauses, and policy documents compliant with UK law
- Offline-first design — works across UK and overseas connectivity environments
- Phase 2: multilingual support (Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, French, Mandarin, Spanish)

### 3. AI-Driven Audit Simulator

An analytics module that replicates regulator expectations:

- Scores client readiness across 10 compliance dimensions
- Produces a colour-coded "risk thermometer"
- Generates an evidence repository with links to source documentation
- Recommends a prioritised remediation timeline

### 4. Consulting and Expert Marketplace

A network of certified regulatory consultants available on-demand through the platform:

- Certified SRS report reviews
- ESG readiness audits
- Investor disclosure preparation
- R&D road-mapping sessions
- Affordable project-based pricing: £1,500–£3,000 per audit

### 5. Learning Engine and Knowledge Graph

A compliance education platform with:

- Gamified compliance modules and micro-certifications
- "Compliance Badges" — measurable credentials for investor due diligence
- Webinars and bootcamps with UK accelerators (TechNation, Foundervine, Startup Bootcamp)

### 6. Data and Reporting API Hub

RESTful API integration hooks for:

- Accounting platforms (Xero, QuickBooks)
- HR and workforce systems
- Carbon-tracking tools
- Third-party ESG data providers
