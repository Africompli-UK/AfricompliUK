## Why January 2027 Changes Everything

UK **Sustainability Reporting Standards (SRS) 1 and 2** become mandatory for qualifying businesses as of **1 January 2027**.

- **SRS 1** requires entities to disclose information on sustainability-related risks and opportunities material to primary users of their financial reports.
- **SRS 2** requires disclosure of climate-related risks and opportunities.

Other relevant obligations include:
- **Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR)** — applies to UK-incorporated firms above certain size thresholds
- **TCFD Requirements** — mandatory for large PLCs
- **EU CSRD (in scope for future expansion)** — applies to large entities above 250 employees

The reporting deadline creates a defined, time-bound demand window. Businesses that are not ready by January 2027 face penalties, exclusion from public tenders, and loss of investor confidence.

## Direct vs Indirect Exposure

A critical insight driving AfriCompli's market thesis:

**Direct exposure** applies to businesses large enough to meet mandatory reporting thresholds (typically 250+ employees, or specific revenue/asset criteria under the Companies Act 2006).

**Indirect exposure** applies to a much broader population of SMEs that:
- Supply services or goods to UK-listed or regulated companies
- Receive ESG data requests from banks, investors, or procurement teams
- Are part of value chains where larger entities must report on their supply chain's sustainability practices

This indirect exposure is the primary compliance challenge for the majority of AfriCompli's target market — and the gap that most existing ESG platforms entirely miss.
